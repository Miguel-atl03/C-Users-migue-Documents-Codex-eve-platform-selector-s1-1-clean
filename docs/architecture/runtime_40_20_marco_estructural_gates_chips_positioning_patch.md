## Proposed Structural Rules

1. Gates are hard regulatory controls.
2. Chips are cognitive auxiliary mechanisms.
3. Chips may interpret, structure, suggest, or prepare.
4. Gates block, authorize candidate, preserve boundary, and govern output.
5. A chip cannot create production authority.
6. A chip cannot write Supabase real.
7. A chip cannot issue final diagnosis without a gate-authorized downstream stage.
8. Gate candidates are not real activation.
9. Gate > Chip in every conflict.
